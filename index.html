Our Story

A Passion Born in a Family Kitchen

In 2016, Chef Marco Rivera returned to his hometown with one mission: to create a restaurant that felt both world-class and genuinely local. What started as a 30-seat neighborhood bistro has grown into a beloved institution.

We believe exceptional food starts long before it reaches the plate. That's why we're out at the farmers' market every Thursday morning, forging relationships with the people who grow what we cook.
